# Roadmap — My Daily Log

## ✅ v1.0 — MVP (shipped)

- Month / Week / Day calendar with sticker piles & peek popover
- Drag & drop (desktop) + tap & long-press drag (mobile)
- Default + custom stickers (emoji / image / name-tag), edit & hide any sticker
- Meal lanes, quantities, entry notes, day notes
- AI: daily greeting, sticker generation, nutrition estimate (Claude-hosted)
- Sounds, drop animation, confetti; mute toggle
- CSV export (incl. day notes), JSON backup/restore, backup nudges
- 2026 liquid-glass theme, avatar logo, tweakable accent/week-start/sticker size

## v1.1 — Insight & stickiness (next)

- [ ] Weekly summary card: streak, most-used stickers, water/exercise counts
- [ ] Monthly AI recap in the daily-greeting voice
- [ ] Visible streak counter with milestone celebrations
- [ ] Search/filter: "show me all 🏃 days"

## v1.2 — Sharing
